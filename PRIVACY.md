# Privacy Policy: Bot Meter for X

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

Bot Meter for X does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data.

**What the extension reads.** On x.com, the extension reads account information that
X has already loaded into the page (post counts, account IDs, follower counts) in order to show a
posts-per-day badge next to each account. This happens entirely inside your browser.

**What the extension sends.** Nothing. The extension makes no network requests of its own and has no
server. It does not use analytics or tracking of any kind.

**What the extension stores.** Only your settings (on/off, thresholds, and how to treat likely bots),
saved with Chrome's `storage.sync` so they follow your Chrome profile. No browsing data, account
data, or post content is stored.

**Third parties.** None. No data is shared with anyone.
