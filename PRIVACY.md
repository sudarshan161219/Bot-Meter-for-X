# Privacy Policy: Bot Meter for X

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

Bot Meter for X does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data.

**What the extension reads.** On x.com, the extension reads information that X has already loaded
into the page: post counts, account IDs, follower counts, X's own "sensitive content" flags, which
posts are ads, the headings of X's sidebar boxes ("Who to follow", trends, Premium, Live), and,
when you open an account's "About this account" panel, the country and handle-change count X shows
there (kept in memory for that visit only).
If "Also match adult words" is on, it also checks post text and display names against a short
built-in word list. With the Reply Filter on, it reads the text and links of the replies under a
post you open, to judge whether each looks low-effort (a stock phrase, emoji only, copy-pasted,
promotion) or matches a keyword you muted. It uses all of this only to show a posts-per-day badge
next to each account, to label, blur or hide likely adult content, and to flag, collapse or hide
replies. This happens entirely inside your browser; post text is kept in memory while the page is
open and is never saved or sent.

**What the extension sends.** Nothing. The extension makes no network requests of its own and has no
server. It does not use analytics or tracking of any kind.

**What the extension stores.** Only your settings (on/off, thresholds, how to treat likely bots,
which posts to check, how to treat adult content, what to hide, and the Reply Filter's mode,
sensitivity, never-filter list of handles and muted keywords), saved with Chrome's `storage.sync`
so they follow your Chrome profile. No browsing data, account data, or post content is stored.

**Third parties.** None. No data is shared with anyone.
