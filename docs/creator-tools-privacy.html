Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

In short: Creator Tools for X sends nothing anywhere and has no server. Bookmarks you save with Bookmark Brain stay in your browser on this device until you delete them.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Creator Tools for X browser extension ("Creator Tools", "the extension"), made by Sudarshan (@buildwithSud), as published on the Chrome Web Store and in its public source repository. Bot Meter for X has its own policy.

What the extension reads

The extension runs only on x.com. There it reads what X has already loaded into the page you're viewing:

  • posts: their text, links, photo and video addresses, counts (likes, reposts, replies, views) and dates
  • their authors: display name, handle and profile picture address

It uses these to build thread exports (Markdown, image carousel, PDF, plain text) and, while you're on x.com/i/bookmarks, to save your bookmarks. It makes no requests to X's API and does not read your direct messages, your password, your X login session, or any page outside x.com.

What the extension stores

Everything below stays on this device:

  • Bookmark Brain: the bookmarked posts that loaded while you were on your bookmarks page (text, author, date, counts, link and media addresses), plus the tags, notes and tag rules you add. They live in the extension's own database (IndexedDB) in your browser profile, are not synced between devices, and are never sent anywhere. Delete them any time with "Delete all data" on the Bookmark Brain page, or by removing the extension.
  • Settings: the Thread Exporter and Bookmark Brain switches, saved with Chrome's storage.sync so they follow your Chrome profile, and your last export format and theme, saved on this device.
  • PDF view: when you export a thread as PDF, its posts are held in Chrome's in-memory session storage just long enough for the print view to open, then removed.

What the extension sends

Nothing. Creator Tools has no server and includes no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code. Exports are files you download or text you copy; where they go is up to you.

To show pictures, the Bookmark Brain page, the PDF view and the carousel header load images from X's image server (pbs.twimg.com), the same way x.com does. No information about you is added to those requests, and nothing is sent anywhere else.

Sharing and selling data

Nothing is collected by or sent to the developer or anyone else, so there is nothing to share or sell. No data is transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness.

Children

Creator Tools does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version ever changes how data is handled, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date, and the change will be described in the extension's release notes.
