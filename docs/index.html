How many posts a day is too many?

Bot Meter puts each account's average posts per day right next to its name in your X feed. A person posts a few times a day. An account that has averaged 400 a day since it was created is almost certainly a bot.

How it works

No API key, no login, and no requests of its own. It reads what X has already loaded.

posts per day=total posts÷days since the account joined

  1. Reads what's already there

    X sends each author's post count and account ID to your browser along with every post. Bot Meter reads them as they arrive.

  2. Works out the account's age

    Account IDs since 2016 carry their creation time, so the join date is exact. Older accounts are estimated to within a few months, marked with ≈.

  3. Badges every author

    A badge appears next to each @handle in your timeline, replies, quotes, search and profiles. Hover it for the details.

Your feed, your rules

Label, dim or collapse

Decide what happens to posts from likely bots: just a red badge, faded out, or folded away behind a "Show" button.

Set your own thresholds

"High rate" starts at 50 a day and "likely bot" at 150 by default. Change either number in the popup.

Quiet mode

Only want to hear about the suspicious ones? Turn off badges for normal accounts and only high-rate accounts get marked.

Pick what gets checked

Switch replies, reposts and quoted posts on or off. Anything switched off gets no badge and is never dimmed.

Hide adult content

Posts X flags as sensitive, and adult-spam like "18+" and "OnlyFans" replies, can be labelled, blurred or hidden. On by default.

Declutter your feed

Hide ads, "Who to follow", "What's happening", "Subscribe to Premium" and "Live on X". Each has its own switch, in any language.

Numbers you can picture

An average in whole numbers: 3/day. Quiet accounts show 2/week or 3/month instead of a fraction.

Private by design

Nothing is collected, stored or sent anywhere. Only your settings are saved. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Is a high number proof that an account is a bot?

No. It's a strong signal, not a verdict. News outlets, live-tweeters and people who reply a lot can legitimately post 50–100 times a day. Accounts averaging hundreds a day for months are a different story.

Does it need my X login or an API key?

No. It works with the X page you already have open and never touches your account, messages or password.

Does it slow X down or use data?

No. It makes no network requests; it only reads numbers X has already sent to your browser and adds small badges.

Why is an older account's join date marked ≈?

X doesn't include join dates with posts. For accounts created before 2016, the date is estimated from the account ID and is accurate to within a few months. Newer accounts get an exact date.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Brave and other Chromium browsers, on x.com.
