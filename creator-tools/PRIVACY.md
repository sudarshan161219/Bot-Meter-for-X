# Privacy Policy: Creator Tools for X

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

Creator Tools for X does not collect, sell, or transmit any personal data. It has no server and no
analytics.

**What the extension reads.** Only on x.com, and only what X has already loaded into the page you're
viewing: posts (text, links, photos and video addresses, counts, dates) and their authors (name,
handle, profile picture address). It uses this to build thread exports and, on your bookmarks page,
to save your bookmarks. It does not read your direct messages, your password or your X login
session, and makes no requests to X's API.

**What the extension stores, all on this device:**
- **Bookmark Brain:** the bookmarked posts that loaded while you were on your bookmarks page on X (text,
  author, date, counts, link and media addresses), plus the tags, notes and tag rules you add. They
  are kept in the extension's own database (IndexedDB) in your browser profile. They are not synced
  between devices and are never sent anywhere. Delete them any time with "Delete all data" on the
  Bookmark Brain page, or by removing the extension.
- **Settings:** the two on/off switches, saved with Chrome's `storage.sync` so they follow your
  Chrome profile, and your last export format and theme, saved on this device.
- **PDF view:** when you export a thread as PDF, its posts are held in Chrome's in-memory session
  storage just long enough for the print view to open, then removed.

**What the extension sends.** Nothing. Exports are files you download or text you copy; where they go
is up to you. To show pictures, the Bookmark Brain page, the PDF view and the carousel header load
images from X's image server (pbs.twimg.com), the same way x.com does. No information about you is
added to those requests, and nothing is sent anywhere else.

**Third parties.** None. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.
